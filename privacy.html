Notes privacy policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Your notes and local storage

Notes stores your note text, checklist items, note colors, workspace names, and app preferences on your device. The app does not require an account with the developer. This version includes no advertising, analytics service, or automatic crash-reporting service. We do not sell your notes or use them to train AI models.

Sharing and linked devices

When you choose to share a notebook, its contents synchronize with devices you approve. Synchronization uses encrypted peer connections and encrypted replicated feeds. Approved devices receive owner access: they can read and edit the notebook, invite additional devices, and remove other devices. Device names, member and device identifiers, invitation identifiers, approval decisions, and synchronization metadata are used to establish and manage those connections. Pairing requests use a public mailbox; do not put sensitive information in your device name.

Network infrastructure

Peer discovery uses the Hyperswarm/HyperDHT network and its bootstrap infrastructure to locate devices. Discovery infrastructure and connected peers can see network information such as IP addresses and ports, connection timing, and discovery identifiers. Shared notebook contents travel encrypted. This app does not configure a developer-operated cloud notebook store or blind storage mirror. Your internet or mobile provider and operating system handle connections under their own policies.

Camera and clipboard

Camera access is optional and is used to scan a sharing QR code. Notes does not record or upload camera images. You can paste a sharing code instead. When you choose to copy remaining note text, a sharing code, or a recovery phrase, it is placed on your system clipboard. Other apps or system clipboard services may be able to access that clipboard. Recovery words shown or entered in the app are hidden or cleared when you leave their screen or background the app.

Credentials and recovery

Notes keeps its local storage key in the operating system's secure credential storage. Shared data and credential metadata use encryption; this is not a claim that every file in the app directory is independently encrypted. A recovery phrase restores your member identity, but it does not back up notebook contents. Recovery needs a surviving device with a copy of the notebook and owner approval of the replacement device. If all copies are lost, the words cannot recover the notes.

Retention and deletion

Notes retains local data while it is available in your app storage. Deleting a note removes it from the current notebook view and synchronizes that deletion to connected linked devices. The synchronization system retains operation history, so deleting a note is not a secure erasure of every historical copy. To remove this device's local app data, use your operating system's app storage controls or uninstall the app; system backups may be retained separately. Other devices control their own copies.

Removing a linked device

Removing a device rotates the notebook's encryption and prevents that device from receiving future shared changes. It does not remotely erase content already received. The removed device can keep its cached history in a separate writable private workspace. Changes in that private workspace do not reach the shared notebook.

Backups and transfers

Notes does not provide its own cloud backup or a portable note export in this version. Android system backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for all app data, including notes, preferences, workspace metadata, and credentials. Backup behavior on other platforms depends on the operating system and your settings. Keep a surviving linked device with your notes, and save your recovery words privately.

Support and policy changes

Contact the developer, moromisato, through the support link in Settings → Help and support. The GitHub issue tracker is public, and GitHub processes posts under its own privacy policy. Do not send recovery words, private keys, sharing codes, or private note contents. We may update this policy when the app's behavior changes; the date above identifies this version.
