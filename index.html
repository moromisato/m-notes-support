Help and support

Sharing and synchronization

On the device with your notes, choose Settings → Sharing → Share my notes. On the other device, scan or paste its sharing code. Compare the device code and approve the request on the sharing device. Keep both apps open and connected while notes synchronize. Local editing works offline.

Choosing a workspace

Use Settings → Workspaces to switch between sets of notes or rename them. Joining another notebook keeps your previous notes in their own workspace. Notes written in one workspace do not automatically appear in another.

A device was removed

The removed device can continue writing in a private workspace containing its cached notes. Its changes do not reach the shared notebook, and it cannot receive future shared changes. To link it again, ask an owner for a new sharing code and approval.

Restoring access

Keep another device linked and save your recovery words from Settings → Recovery. On a replacement device, use Restore access with your saved words and a new sharing code from a surviving device. Its owner must approve. If every copy of the notebook is lost, the words alone cannot recover its contents.

Deleting notes and local data

Deleting a note removes it from the current notebook and synchronizes the deletion to linked devices. Operation history and copies retained by other devices are not securely erased. Use your operating system's app storage controls or uninstall Notes to remove local app data. Other devices and system backups manage their copies separately.

When requesting help

Describe what happened, your device model, and your Notes version. Share screenshots only after removing private contents. Never send recovery words, private keys, pairing codes, or sensitive note text. The support issue tracker is public, so everyone can read anything you post there.
